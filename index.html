CHASING THE INVISIBLE LIGHT

I believe photography is less about looking and more about feeling. For me, it is a way to pause time—capturing not just what is seen, but what is experienced in a fleeting moment.

I'm Bara, a photographer drawn to both quiet landscapes and the energy of everyday life. Through my lens, I try to reveal the cinematic beauty hidden in ordinary scenes—the subtle details, the light, and the atmosphere that often go unnoticed.

Based in Indonesia
